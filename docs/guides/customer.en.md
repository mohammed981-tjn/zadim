# Shopping at Zadim

> Last updated: 2026-09-03 — matches the national-address batch.
> This guide describes the store **as it is today**. Anything not built
> yet is said so plainly here, rather than left for you to discover.

Welcome. These few pages explain how to buy from Zadim, and what to do
when a message you weren't expecting appears.

---

## Arabic and English

The store runs in two languages. The language switch (the letters icon)
sits at the top of every page.

Switching keeps you **where you are**: if you're on a product page you
stay on that product rather than being thrown back to the home page.
The address starts with `/ar` or `/en`, so you can share a link in the
language you're reading.

**What you see in English is genuinely English content**, not translated
buttons over Arabic text: product names, descriptions and the home page
sections are all translated, and prices are written `399.00 SAR` rather
than in Arabic-Indic digits.

⚠️ A product whose translation hasn't been added yet **appears under its
Arabic name** on the English page. That is deliberate: an Arabic name
you can copy is more useful than an empty field.

## Browsing and search

**The home page** is a set of sections the store arranges: a banner,
selected products, categories. It may change from one day to the next.

**Search** is at the top of the page. Type it however you like — the
field understands:

- Arabic spelling in all its variants: `ايفون` and `أيفون` find the same
  thing, and so do the two forms of the final t, and alif with or
  without hamza.
- The Latin name: `iPhone` finds what `ايفون` finds.

Search behaves the same way in both languages: type Arabic on the
English page and you'll still get results, with the product name shown
translated.

⚠️ **There is no filtering yet** (by colour or size) on category pages.
Attributes are recorded on every product and you can see them on its
page, but the filter controls haven't been added — and we'd rather not
show a button that does nothing.

## The cart

Press **"Add to cart"** on a product page. Your cart is behind the cart
icon at the top, with the number of items on it.

In the cart you can change quantities and remove what you don't want.
Your cart is kept in this browser, so it survives closing the page and
coming back.

## Prices and VAT

**Every price shown includes 15% VAT.** The number you see on a product
is what you pay.

In the order summary you'll find:

| Line | What it means |
|---|---|
| Items | The products' total, **VAT included** |
| Shipping | The delivery charge, **VAT included** |
| Total | Items + shipping |
| "Prices include 15% VAT — of which …" | **How much of the total is VAT** |

🔴 Note that the VAT line is **a statement, not an addition**: it is part
of the numbers above, not an amount added to them. Items + shipping =
total, with nothing extra.

## Checking out

Three steps: **address**, then **shipping method**, then **review and
pay**.

### Your national address

We ask for the full **Saudi National Address** as registered with Saudi
Post — not a single address line:

| Field | Example | Note |
|---|---|---|
| First and last name | Mohammed Alotaibi | |
| Mobile number | 0555000111 | **How the courier reaches you** |
| Building number | 2743 | Four digits |
| Street name | King Fahd Road | |
| District | Al Olaya | **Couriers rely on it more than the street** |
| City | Riyadh | |
| Postal code | 12211 | Five digits |
| Additional number | 6889 | Four digits — pinpoints your entrance |
| Short address | RRRD2929 | **Optional** |
| Email | | **Optional** — for your order confirmation |

You can find all of these in the **National Address** app or at
[splonline.com.sa](https://splonline.com.sa).

⚠️ **The screen will not move on to shipping until the store accepts
your address.** If a field is missing or a number has the wrong length,
the reason appears under that field — and every wrong field is marked at
once, not one at a time.

Arabic-Indic and Western digits are both accepted (`٢٧٤٣` and `2743` are
the same), and your mobile number is accepted in any format you write it
(`0555…` or `+966555…`).

### Review

The review step shows your final total, and you press **"Confirm
order"**. Pressing it means you agree to pay the amount shown.

### Cash on delivery

Payment today is **cash on delivery** to the courier. You are never
asked for a card or any banking details at any step — so if a site
claiming to be Zadim asks you for them, it isn't us.

⚠️ Electronic payment methods (mada, Apple Pay, Tabby, Tamara) **are not
enabled yet**; they need accounts with the payment providers.

### Your invoice

After confirming, an **electronic invoice** is issued to you, compliant
with the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority's requirements, carrying your
order number.

## Two messages you may see at checkout — and what each means

Neither is a fault. Both protect you.

### "Prices changed"

The price of something in your cart changed between the moment you added
it and the moment you went to pay.

**The important part**: the order is **stopped before any money is taken
from you**, and it is never pushed through at the new price without your
knowledge. The differences are shown to you **line by line**: the price
that was, the price now, the difference, then the new total.

**What to do**: press **"Re-price"** to see the new total, then confirm
if you're happy with it — or go back to the cart and remove whatever you
don't want at its new price.

⚠️ You can't confirm before re-pricing. That's deliberate: you shouldn't
confirm a number you haven't seen.

### "Out of stock"

The quantity you asked for is no longer fully available — someone else
may have bought it in that moment.

You are shown **how many are short for each item**. Press **"Edit
cart"**, reduce the quantity or remove the item, then carry on.

## After you order

You land on a **confirmation page** with your order number and total.

⚠️ **Save that page's link, or your order number.** There are no accounts
at Zadim today and no "my orders" page: checkout is guest checkout, and
your order number is what you quote to us.

## Returns

The return policy is set by the store and may change over time. What
gets checked:

| Condition | What it means |
|---|---|
| **The window** | A number of days from the delivery date. It counts whole days, and **the last day counts in your favour** |
| **Excluded categories** | Some categories can't be returned by their nature |
| **Opened items** | Whether an item is accepted once its packaging is opened |
| **Minimum total** | The order total may need to be above a certain amount |
| **Return shipping** | Paid by the store or by you — per the published policy |

⚠️ **There is no page today for you to request a return yourself.** To
request one, **contact the store** with your order number, and our team
takes it from there.

**What happens once your return arrives**: the goods go into a separate
area that nothing is sold from, a member of staff inspects them and
records the outcome and the reason for it. Nothing goes back on sale
without an inspection signed by a person.

## Quick answers

**Do I need an account?** No. You can buy without registering.

**Do you store my card?** We never ask for a card — payment is on
delivery.

**I opened the store and the home page was empty.** The sections haven't
been set up yet from the store's panel. Try search, or go straight to a
category link.

**I switched language and the prices changed?** No, it's the same
amount. Only the **writing** changes: `٣٩٩٫٠٠ ر.س` in Arabic,
`399.00 SAR` in English.

**The page won't load.** Refresh in a moment; if it keeps happening,
get in touch.
